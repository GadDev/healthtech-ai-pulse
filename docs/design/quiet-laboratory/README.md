## Design decisions

- Editorial titles use a system serif stack beginning with Iowan Old Style or Baskerville, with Georgia as a common fallback. Body copy and interface labels remain sans serif. The exact installed face can vary by device, so line wraps need browser review on target platforms.
- The Daily AI Pulse reading pattern remains: masthead, prominent lead, text-led desk navigation and a centered article column. Category pages show the same story without creating filler for the other desks.
- The article places a direct evidence summary before the reporting and provides jumps to evidence, analysis, sources and the corrections policy.
- The Quiet Laboratory palette keeps ink and dark teal for small text; sage, mist, rose and caramel are restrained surfaces or accents.
- The generated photograph is a contextual still life. Captions distinguish it from the reported study site and participants; the photo does not document clinical results.
