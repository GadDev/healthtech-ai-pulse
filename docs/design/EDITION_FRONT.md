# Edition Front implementation review

The publication uses a dated serif masthead, a story-first homepage, a Categories dropdown routing to the category index and its five editorial desks, and a white reading canvas. Teal rules and compact evidence summaries provide structure. Article navigation jumps to the full evidence record, analysis, original sources and corrections. The mobile layout places the summary before the copy and wraps publication navigation into visible rows.

The Categories disclosure uses native `<details>/<summary>` with a visible chevron and ordinary navigation links. The panel overlays content without moving the masthead or article, and spans the navigation width with side gutters on small screens. Links and the trigger have at least 44px-high targets; the current destination has `aria-current="page"`, while the trigger marks the active category section visually. A small progressive enhancement closes the panel on Escape (returning focus to the trigger), outside click, focus leaving the disclosure, or ordinary link activation. Native opening and all category links remain available without JavaScript. Keep the white canvas, dark teal rule and restrained sage interaction states from Quiet Laboratory.
